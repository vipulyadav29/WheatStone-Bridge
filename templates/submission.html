Abstract

Wheatstone Bridge is a wheat-only disease diagnosis platform that allows users to upload wheat leaf images, identify likely disease classes, understand visible symptoms, and review result history through a modern web interface.

Problem Statement

Early disease identification in wheat crops is important for crop protection, but manual diagnosis may be delayed or difficult. This platform aims to simplify the first diagnosis step.

Objectives

Core goals of the project

01

Detect diseases from wheat leaf images

Keep the system specific to wheat so the diagnosis scope remains focused and understandable.

02

Classify healthy and diseased conditions

The current platform targets Healthy, Rust, Leaf Blight, and Powdery Mildew.

03

Provide a simple web-based interface

Users can upload images, view results, browse diseases, and access diagnosis history.

04

Support future ML model integration

The running app is ready for a trained model later, while remaining stable in the current environment.
