Platform Overview

Wheatstone Bridge turns wheat leaf images into a clear, diagnosis-first experience.

Wheatstone Bridge combines image upload, wheat disease classification, result presentation, and disease awareness content in one wheat-focused platform.

Why It Matters

Why a wheat diagnosis platform is useful

Late identification

Farmers often notice symptoms after disease spread has already affected crop quality and yield.

Manual dependency

Traditional diagnosis may depend on expert availability, which is not always easy to access quickly.

Need for clarity

A good diagnosis platform should explain the process simply, not just show a model output without context.

Core Features

What Wheatstone Bridge is designed to deliver

01

Detect wheat diseases from leaf images

Focus only on wheat to keep the platform specific and useful for one crop problem space.

02

Classify four important outcomes

Healthy, Rust, Leaf Blight, and Powdery Mildew are the current classes in the system.

03

Provide a user-friendly website

The interface is designed so users can move from image upload to disease understanding without confusion.

04

Support real-model integration

The frontend and backend structure are ready for connecting a trained model later.

Product Direction

A cleaner website direction inspired by modern crop diagnosis products

Diagnosis-first design

Important actions such as crop diagnosis, disease browsing, and contact stay visible and simple.

Knowledge-rich experience

The platform combines prediction flow with disease awareness, making the result more meaningful.
