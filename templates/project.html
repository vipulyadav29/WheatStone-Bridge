How It Works

From wheat leaf photo to clear crop-health guidance.

WheatStone Bridge keeps the diagnosis journey simple: upload a wheat leaf image, confirm the leaf is suitable, view the likely condition, and keep a record for later review.

Why It Matters

Early wheat disease screening helps protect crop health

Spot symptoms earlier

Rust, blight, and mildew signs can appear on leaves before wider crop damage is obvious.

Reduce confusion

Clear disease guidance helps users understand what the visible symptoms may indicate.

Review past scans

Saved records make it easier to compare recent wheat leaf checks over time.

Scan Flow

A clean four-step diagnosis experience

01

Upload a wheat leaf

Choose a clear photo with the leaf visible and enough light for screening.

02

Validate the image

Non-wheat or unclear uploads are flagged before diagnosis so results stay relevant.

03

Read the condition

The scan returns the likely wheat leaf condition with confidence and simple guidance.

04

Track the result

Valid wheat scans are saved in records so users can review previous diagnosis activity.

Designed For Clarity

Diagnosis-first layout

The main scan action, result card, disease guide, and records are easy to find from any page.

Human-friendly results

Each scan gives a condition name, confidence level, and short guidance that is easy to understand.
